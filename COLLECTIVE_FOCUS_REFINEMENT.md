# Khula Collective - Staying True to Our Mission

## 🎯 Core Mission Reminder

**We are NOT individual investment accounts.**
**We ARE a collective savings pool for group investment opportunities.**

---

## ✅ What Khula Collective IS

### The True Purpose:
1. **Pool Resources Together** - Everyone contributes R300/month to ONE shared pot
2. **Invest as a Group** - The collective pot is invested in opportunities we couldn't access alone
3. **Share Returns Equally** - Investment returns benefit the entire collective proportionally
4. **Grow Together** - Success is measured by the collective pot growth, not individual accounts
5. **Community Decision-Making** - Major investment decisions made together

### The Power of Collective:
```
Individual: R300/month = R3,600/year
  → Limited investment options
  → Lower returns (4-6% savings account)
  → No negotiating power

Collective: 20 members × R300 = R6,000/month = R72,000/year
  → Access to RSA Retail Bonds (min R50k)
  → Higher returns (8-11%)
  → Bulk investment advantages
  → Professional management options
```

## ❌ What We're NOT

### We Are NOT:
1. ❌ Individual investment accounts
2. ❌ Personal savings accounts with separate investments
3. ❌ A platform where each person invests their own money separately
4. ❌ A place where members compete against each other
5. ❌ A service where you can withdraw your portion anytime

### We ARE:
1. ✅ A collective pot where everyone's contributions are pooled
2. ✅ A community that invests together in shared opportunities
3. ✅ A group that benefits from collective bargaining power
4. ✅ A platform where members support each other's consistency
5. ✅ A long-term commitment to group wealth building
