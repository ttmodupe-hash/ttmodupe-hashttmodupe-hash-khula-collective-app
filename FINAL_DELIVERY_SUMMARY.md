## 💡 Core Principles Maintained

### 1. Collective Pooling
- All members contribute to ONE shared pot
- R300 × 20 members = R6,000/month
- R72,000/year collective investment fund

### 2. Proportional Sharing
- Returns distributed based on contribution percentage
- Fair and transparent
- Example: 5% contribution = 5% of returns

### 3. Democratic Decision-Making
- Major investments voted on by members
- Everyone's voice matters equally
- Community-driven strategy

### 4. Community Support
- Help members who struggle
- No competition, just support
- Celebrate collective milestones

### 5. Transparency
- See total collective pot
- Know where it's invested
- Track collective returns
- Understand your share
