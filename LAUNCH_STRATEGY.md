## 🎨 Marketing Channels

### 1. **Social Media** (Primary)

#### Facebook:
- **Target Audience**: 25-45 year olds, interested in finance, savings, investment
- **Content Types**: 
  - Success stories (3x/week)
  - Investment tips (2x/week)
  - Member spotlights (1x/week)
  - Live Q&A sessions (1x/month)
- **Budget**: R500/month for ads
- **Goal**: 500 followers, 50 signups in 3 months

#### Instagram:
- **Target Audience**: 22-35 year olds, aspirational savers
- **Content Types**:
  - Infographics (daily)
  - Stories with polls/questions (daily)
  - Reels showing growth (2x/week)
  - Member testimonials (1x/week)
- **Budget**: R300/month for ads
- **Goal**: 1000 followers, 30 signups in 3 months

- **Budget**: Organic only
- **Goal**: 300 followers, 20 signups in 3 months
