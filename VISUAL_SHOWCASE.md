## 💡 Design Principles

### Visual Hierarchy
1. **Large Headlines** - Grab attention
2. **Clear Sections** - Easy scanning
3. **Strategic Color** - Guide the eye
4. **White Space** - Reduce clutter
5. **Consistent Spacing** - Professional look

### Color Usage
- **Purple Gradients** - Premium, trustworthy
- **Green Accents** - Growth, success
- **Yellow Highlights** - Energy, attention
- **White Space** - Clean, modern

### Typography
- **Bold Headlines** - Clear messaging
- **Regular Body** - Easy reading
- **Consistent Sizing** - Professional hierarchy
